About the College

ABES Engineering College is one of the leading engineering colleges in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The college offers quality education, modern laboratories, experienced faculty, and excellent placement opportunities.

Departments

1. Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

Focuses on programming, software development, AI, machine learning, data structures, and cloud computing.

2. Information Technology (IT)

Provides knowledge in web development, networking, cybersecurity, and database management.

3. Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

Covers communication systems, embedded systems, IoT, and VLSI.

4. Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)

Deals with electrical machines, power systems, and renewable energy.

5. Mechanical Engineering (ME)

Focuses on manufacturing, robotics, CAD/CAM, and thermal engineering.

6. Data Science

Offers education in data analysis, artificial intelligence, machine learning, Python, and big data analytics.
